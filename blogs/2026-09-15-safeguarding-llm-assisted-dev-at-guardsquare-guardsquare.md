This post is not meant to tell you how to use large language models (LLMs) or to claim we've found the right approach. As a cybersecurity company working with particularly sensitive IP, our decision to use LLMs for development was never just about productivity. The broader enthusiasm around LLMs was not itself a reason for us to adopt them quickly.
