Over the last week, multiple sources in Japan have identified a widening outbreak of data leaks via mobile API attacks. This includes detailed reports from an independent incident response team ( JPCERT Coordination Center ), analysis by security researchers at Macnica , a breach disclosure from one of the affected organizations ( Park24 ), as well as multiple news stories from mainstream press in the region and international tech industry publications.
